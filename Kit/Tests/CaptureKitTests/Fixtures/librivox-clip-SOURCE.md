# librivox-clip

Utterances 1272-128104-0000 to -0007 from the LibriSpeech corpus (dev-clean),
joined with 0.4 s pauses and encoded as 16 kHz mono AAC. The audio comes from a
public-domain LibriVox recording; `librivox-clip.txt` is the LibriSpeech
reference transcript, lowercased.

LibriSpeech: Vassil Panayotov, Guoguo Chen, Daniel Povey, Sanjeev Khudanpur,
"LibriSpeech: an ASR corpus based on public domain audio books", ICASSP 2015.
https://www.openslr.org/12 — licensed CC BY 4.0.
Taken from the Hugging Face dataset hf-internal-testing/librispeech_asr_dummy.
